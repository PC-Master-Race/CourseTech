What AI Actually Is

Before you use AI at work, let's get clear on what it really is, in plain language.

A video walkthrough is coming for this lesson.You can work through everything below right now. No video needed.

On this page

  1. Three things people mix up
  2. How we got here, a short history
  3. What generative AI is
  4. What a language model actually does

Part 1

Three things people mix up

Lesson

Software, automation, and AI are not the same

Let's clear the air before we touch a single tool. People throw around the word "AI" for almost anything a computer does, and that mix-up is where a lot of confusion starts. Here is how I want you to keep three ideas apart.

Term What it does Everyday example
Software Follows fixed instructions a person wrote A calculator app
Automation Repeats a set task on its own, by the rules A spell-checker using a fixed dictionary
Artificial intelligence Learns patterns from lots of examples A writing assistant that learns what "sounds right"

The heart of it: software and automation follow rules. AI learns from examples. A plain spell-checker looks words up in a list. An AI writing assistant learned from billions of sentences what good writing looks like. That is a different kind of machine, and it behaves differently.

Activity · try it

Sort three tools

We just sorted three kinds of tool: plain software, automation, and AI. Let's try that with tools from your own life. Pick three apps or gadgets you have used recently, and for each one write down:

  1. What is its main job?
  2. Does it follow fixed rules, or does it seem to learn from examples and adapt?

You do not need to get these exactly right. The point is to start noticing the difference we just talked about.

Part 2

How we got here, a short history

Lesson

From rule books to conversation

You do not need the full history of AI to use it well. But knowing the arc helps you understand why today's tools work the way they do, and why they sometimes fail.

  • Rules-based AI (1950s to 1980s). Early systems were giant "if this, then that" rule books written by hand. Smart inside their narrow lane, but they broke the moment something new came up. They could not learn.
  • Machine learning (1980s to 2010s). Instead of coding every rule, we started showing the system lots of examples and letting it find the patterns itself. Show it a million emails, some spam and some not, and it learns the difference. That is how your spam filter works today.
  • Large language models (2017 to now). These are the systems behind ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude. For the first time, you can just type a plain-English request and get a useful response. No coding required. That is exactly why this course exists.

A quick note on terms. A large language model, or LLM, is an AI trained on a huge amount of text so it can read and write human language.

Part 3

What generative AI is

Lesson

The kind of AI that makes new things

You have probably heard the phrase "generative AI" a lot lately. It is worth two minutes, because it is the family that today's most talked-about tools belong to.

Here is the plain version. For a long time, most AI did one job: it sorted or scored things that already existed. Is this email spam, yes or no? Which of these photos has a cat in it? Useful work, but the AI was picking from options, not making anything.

Generative AI flipped that. It creates brand-new content that did not exist a moment ago, based on a request you type in. Ask for a thank-you note and it writes one. Ask for a picture of a calm waiting room and it paints one. The word "generative" just means it generates, it produces.

A quick note on terms. Generative AI is AI that creates new content, like text, images, or audio, from a plain-language request you give it.

Lesson

It shows up in more than text

Text is where most workplace value lives right now, and it is where this course spends most of its time. But generative AI is not only about writing. The same basic idea powers a few different kinds of tools.

You ask for... The tool makes... Examples you may have heard of
Writing or answers Text ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude
A picture from a description An image Gemini, Canva, Adobe Firefly
A voice or a bit of music Audio Various voice and music tools

One thing to hold onto: because these tools make something new every time, you can ask the same thing twice and get two different results. That is normal. It is also why the quality of your request matters so much, which is the whole point of the next topic on prompting.

Activity · try it

Spot the generative tool

Think back over the last week or two. See if you can name one time you used, or watched someone use, a tool that made something new for them.

  1. What did it create? Text, a picture, audio, or something else?
  2. What did the person ask for to get it?

If nothing comes to mind yet, that is fine. You will be using one before this course is over.

Part 4

What a language model actually does

Lesson

It predicts the next word

This part surprises most people, and understanding it will make you a sharper user right away.

When you send a message to ChatGPT or Gemini, here is what really happens: the model reads your text and predicts, one word at a time, what is most likely to come next. It learned those patterns from an enormous amount of writing.

A few things follow from that:

  • It is not looking things up in a database, unless a web-search feature is switched on.
  • It does not "know" facts the way you look one up in a textbook.
  • It produces text that is likely to be correct. Most of the time that is genuinely useful. Sometimes it is confidently wrong. We cover that in "When AI Gets It Wrong."

Lesson

A way to picture it

Picture a coworker who has read almost everything ever published, in dozens of languages, and can talk about nearly any subject. Incredibly well-read. Very helpful.

But that coworker was not in the room for your last policy change, and they cannot see your files or your customer records. So they are a great collaborator, not an all-knowing oracle. Bring that mindset to every chat with an AI tool and you will already be ahead of most people.
