Welcome to Tutorial Hub

July 24, 2026 · Mr. Perez

A quick hello, what this site is for, and what's here already.

Hi, and welcome. I built this site to keep all of my lessons in one calm, easy place that you can come back to any time, on your own schedule.

If you have landed here from one of my classes, you already know the drill. If you found it some other way, you are just as welcome. Everything here is written for real people who are getting comfortable with technology, in plain language, with no rush and no silly questions.

What's here so far

Right now there are three courses:

  • AI at Work, a full set of lessons on using AI tools well and knowing when not to, from what AI actually is all the way through agents and audio.
  • Office Productivity, starting with Basic Excel.
  • Devices & Digital Life, starting with iPhone basics.

Each course has its own page with the lessons laid out in order, and every lesson breaks down into short parts you can take one at a time.

What this news page is for

I'll use this space to let you know when I add a new lesson, share a quick tip worth trying, or point you to something useful. Nothing long, just short notes when there is something worth telling you.

Thanks for being here. Poke around, start anywhere, and come back whenever you like.
