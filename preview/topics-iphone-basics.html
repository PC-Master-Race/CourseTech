Mobile Tech: iPhone Basics

Your iPhone's buttons, home screen, and how to make it truly yours. We'll go slow.

Part 1

Before we begin

Lesson

Let's get set up

Welcome. I am so glad you are here. Before we dive in, let's take a moment so today goes smoothly. Check these four things:

  • Your phone is charged. The screen should light up when you press a button.
  • Your glasses are on if you use them. Everything today is on that small screen.
  • This guide is open in front of you. You are reading it, so that one is done.
  • Your sound is on. Look at the left edge of the phone. If you see a little switch showing orange, the sound is off. We will come back to that.

You are all set. Let's have some fun.

Part 2

The three buttons you can feel

Lesson

Your physical controls

Your iPhone has three real buttons you can feel with your fingers. Let's learn where they are and what they do.

The Side Button (right edge). This is your on and off switch. You can feel it with your right thumb when you hold the phone normally.

  • Press it once to wake the screen up.
  • Press it once while the screen is on to lock the phone (the screen goes dark to save battery).
  • Press and hold for two seconds to bring up the power-off menu.

The Volume Buttons (left edge). Two buttons, one above the other.

  • Top button turns the sound up.
  • Bottom button turns the sound down.
  • When the phone is ringing, pressing either one quiets the ring right away.

Lesson

How to turn the phone all the way off

Sometimes you need to fully power down. Here is the safe way:

  1. Press and hold the Side Button (right) and the Volume Up button (left, top) at the same time.
  2. Hold both for about two seconds.
  3. A screen appears with a white bar that says "slide to power off" near the top.
  4. Put your finger on that white bar and slide it from left to right.

To turn it back on, press and hold the Side Button until the Apple logo appears.

Activity · try it

Practice the buttons

Try each one slowly. There is no wrong move here.

  1. Press the Side Button once to lock the screen, then once more to wake it.
  2. Press Volume Up a few times, then Volume Down. Listen for the change.
  3. Find the little switch on the top left. Flip it and watch for the orange color.

Do each one twice so your fingers remember where the buttons are.

Part 3

Your home screen

Lesson

What all those little pictures are

When your phone is unlocked, you land on the home screen. Those little pictures are called apps. An app is just a program that does one job, like Phone, Messages, Camera, or Weather.

  • Tap an app once (a quick, light touch) to open it.
  • Swipe left or right (slide one finger across the glass) to see more pages of apps.
  • Swipe up from the very bottom edge to close an app and come back home.

The row of apps at the very bottom stays put on every page. That is called the Dock, and it is a good place to keep the apps you use most.

Activity · try it

Open and close an app

Let's build the most important habit on the phone:

  1. Tap the Camera app to open it.
  2. Look around. Do not worry about taking a photo yet.
  3. Swipe up from the bottom edge to come back to the home screen.

Do this with two more apps of your choice. Open, look, come home. That is the rhythm of using a phone.

Part 4

Making the screen easier to see

Lesson

Bigger text and more

This is one of my favorite parts, because it makes the phone yours. If the text feels small, we can fix it.

  1. Open the Settings app (the gray gear icon).
  2. Tap Display & Brightness.
  3. Tap Text Size and drag the slider to the right to make words bigger.

While you are in Settings, look for Accessibility. Inside there you can turn on Bold Text, or Zoom to magnify the screen. Try them and keep whatever helps. You should not have to squint.
