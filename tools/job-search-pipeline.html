The Automated Job Search Pipeline

Hand your job search to an AI assistant. It finds openings that fit you, scores each one, and drafts tailored resumes and cover letters built to get past the resume screener, while you stay in control and apply yourself. It's a template: fill in your own details and run it.

What it does for you

  • Finds the right openings. Searches the job boards and company career pages you choose, using your keywords.
  • Scores every job 0 to 100 against your background, so you focus on the best fits first.
  • Writes ATS-ready resumes. Mirrors each posting's exact keywords, uses proven bullet formulas, and runs a red-flag checklist so a real person actually sees your resume.
  • Drafts a tailored cover letter for each strong match, in your own voice.
  • Keeps a running log of everything it finds, so nothing slips through the cracks.
  • Stays safe and honest. It never invents credentials, never auto-applies, and treats web pages as data, not instructions.

You will need an AI assistant that can read your files and browse the web to run the full version. A lighter copy-and-paste version works too: paste in a posting, and it scores it and drafts your materials.
