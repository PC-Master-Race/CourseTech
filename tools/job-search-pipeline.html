The Automated Job Search Pipeline

Let an AI do the heavy lifting of your job search. It finds openings that fit you, ranks the best ones, and writes a tailored resume and cover letter for each, the kind built to get past the software that screens resumes. You stay in charge and do the applying yourself.

What this is, in plain terms

Think of this as a set of instructions you hand to an AI assistant. You fill in a few blanks about yourself, paste it in, and the AI goes to work: it searches the job sites you pick, scores each opening against your background, and writes you a resume and cover letter for the best matches. It never applies for you. It gets everything ready so you can review it and hit send yourself.

What it does for you

  • Finds the right openings on the job sites and company pages you choose.
  • Ranks each job from 0 to 100 so you spend your time on the best fits.
  • Writes resumes that get read. It matches the exact words from each posting, so the resume-screening software does not toss you out before a person ever sees it.
  • Drafts a cover letter for each strong match, in your own voice.
  • Keeps a running list of everything it finds, so nothing gets lost.
  • Plays it safe and honest. It never makes up experience and never applies on your behalf.

How to use it

  1. Get an AI helper. You need an AI assistant that can read your files and go online, then give it these instructions. If you just want to try it, you can also paste in a single job posting and let the AI work on that one.
  2. Gather your stuff. Put your current resume, any older ones, and a note about your work history in one folder, so the AI can see your real background.
  3. Fill in the blanks. In the instructions below, anywhere you see something in [square brackets], type your own answer. It only takes a few minutes.
  4. Paste it in and let it run. Hand the instructions to your AI helper. It finds jobs, ranks them, and writes your materials. You review everything and apply yourself.

The blanks you'll fill in

You do not need to understand every line below. You only need to answer a few simple things:

  • Your goal what kind of job you want, full-time or part-time, and where.
  • Your background the folder that holds your resumes and work history.
  • Your job sites the websites you want it to search.
  • Anything to skip jobs or personal details you do not want included.
