Hi, I am your instructor. I teach vocational re-entry courses, things like Excel,
basic computing, mobile technology, and using AI at work. I built this site to keep
all of my lessons in one calm, easy place that belongs to us, separate from Canvas.

**Who this is for.** These lessons are written for adults who are getting comfortable
with technology, including folks who feel like they are starting from scratch. If you
have ever thought "everyone else seems to get this but me," you are exactly who I had
in mind. There are no silly questions here.

**How the lessons work.** Every topic is broken into short parts, and each part mixes
three kinds of blocks:

- A **Lesson** block is the main teaching. Read it at your own pace.
- An **Activity** block is a small hands-on task. This is where the learning sticks.
- A **Check your understanding** block links out to a short quiz you can take.

You will see a color, a label, and an icon on each one, so you always know what you
are looking at.

**A few promises from me.** I write in plain language and explain any technical word
the first time it shows up. I would rather show you a clear example than bury you in
theory. And I keep adding new topics, so check back.

If a lesson ever feels unclear, that is on the lesson, not on you. Tell me, and I will
fix it.
